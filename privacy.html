Mythbound Studio: Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · Mythbound

What this is

Mythbound Studio is a private desktop tool used only by the operator of the Mythbound YouTube channel. It publishes the channel's own videos and reads the channel's own performance data. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

Google and YouTube data it accesses

When the channel's owner signs in with Google and grants permission, Mythbound Studio may access:

  • the Mythbound channel's basic information;
  • uploading videos to that channel, and the channel's video list and video details;
  • the channel's branding settings (banner, watermark, description, keywords) and its playlists;
  • YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's videos (views, watch time, retention, subscribers, traffic sources).

How the data is used

Only to publish the channel's videos, keep its branding and playlists up to date, and measure how videos perform so that future videos can be better. The data isn't sold or shared with anyone, isn't transferred to third parties, and isn't used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Sign-in tokens and analytics data are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is stored on a server run by Mythbound.

YouTube API Services

Mythbound Studio uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working. Locally stored data is deleted on request, or when the tool is retired.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Mythbound channel's About page on YouTube.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
